Topic Guide

Special Education & Individual Instruction

Special education is the area in which Vincent B. Troiano spent most of his teaching career: 27 of 36 years. It is also one of the deepest subject areas in The Silent Voice in Education.

What the book covers

The official publisher table of contents devotes multiple chapters directly to special education: “Special Education: Basis and Reform,” “Classroom Management in Special Education,” “Special Education as a Bureaucracy,” “IEP as Legal Document vs Classroom Teacher Day-to-Day Evaluations,” and “Individual Instruction.” The complete table of contents is available from the publisher.

The practitioner perspective

Troiano’s special education career began in self-contained classes with students identified at the time as Emotionally Disturbed. His writing emphasizes the difference between a formal plan or policy and the continuous observation, adjustment, and judgment required when teaching an individual student.

Troiano’s experience in special education informs his broader argument that classroom judgment and knowledge of individual students should have a meaningful place in education policy.
